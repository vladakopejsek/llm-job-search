# Pay targets (example)

Copy to data/pay.md (gitignored) and put in your own numbers. Amounts are gross;
the currency is any ISO code; the period is year, month, day or hour.
Contract floor/stretch default to the contract target, employment floor/stretch
to the employment target.

- employment.floor: 60,000 EUR / year
- employment.target: 75,000 EUR / year
- employment.stretch: 90,000 EUR / year
- contract.target: 450 EUR / day
